[b]Fair Duelist Difficulty[/b]

Shorter, deadly fights with a little more room for mistakes. By default, enemies have 25% less health than Fair, deal Fair damage, and your combat actions cost Fair stamina. Enemy aggression keeps the Duelist feel.

The existing Duelist option is renamed [b]Fair Duelist[/b]. You can keep this setup or adjust it to your taste.

[b]How do I change my difficulty settings?[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Install [url=https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases]UE4SS[/url] and this mod, then launch the game once.[/*]
[*]Press [b]Win + R[/b], paste the path below, and press Enter. Open the file in Notepad.[/*]
[/list]
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Dawnwalker\Saved\Config\FairDuelist.ini[/code]
Remove the [b];[/b] at the start of a setting to enable your change, then edit its value. Save the file and restart the game. Keep both [b]RPG Difficulty[/b] and [b]Action Difficulty[/b] set to [b]Fair Duelist[/b].

[list]
[*][b]referenceDifficulty[/b] — choose Story, Fair, Challenging, or Duelist as your starting point.[/*]
[*][b]enemyHealthMultiplier[/b] — lower it to kill enemies in fewer hits.[/*]
[*][b]enemyDamageMultiplier[/b] — lower it to take less damage.[/*]
[*][b]staminaCostMultiplier[/b] — lower it to spend less stamina on combat actions.[/*]
[*][b]enemyAggressionMultiplier[/b] — lower it to give enemies longer waits between attacks.[/*]
[/list]
[b]1.0[/b] uses your chosen difficulty's value. [b]0.75[/b] means 25% less health, damage or stamina cost. For aggression, 0.75 makes attack cooldowns about 33% longer. Values can range from 0.1 to 5.0.

For example, choose [b]Fair[/b], set health to [b]0.75[/b], and leave the other multipliers at [b]1.0[/b] for shorter fights with Fair damage, stamina costs and aggression. Changing the reference affects all multipliers.

[b]Default INI values[/b]
[code]referenceDifficulty=Duelist
enemyHealthMultiplier=0.833333333333
enemyDamageMultiplier=0.625
staminaCostMultiplier=0.571428571429
enemyAggressionMultiplier=1.0[/code]
Settings you leave commented out use the mod's defaults. Your personal file is kept between mod updates.

Aggression adjusts attack cooldowns and how enemies attack together. It does not remove red/unblockable attacks or change attack animations, parry timing or boss health phases. You can still change directional indicators and other options in the game's menu.

[b]Requirements[/b]
[url=https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases]UE4SS[/url] develop build 97b7e501c or compatible is needed to use the INI. The default preset also works without UE4SS.

[b]Install[/b]
Install through Vortex. To update, replace the existing Fair Duelist mod with the new archive and deploy, then restart the game.

[b]Compatibility and conflicts[/b]
Do not combine this with another mod replacing the same difficulty configuration or text tables unless you have chosen which version should win. Fair Duelist must win for its balance and wording to apply.
Affected assets:
[list]
[*]/Game/_Dawnwalker/Combat/DA_DifficultyConfig
[*]/Game/_Dawnwalker/Combat/StringTables/ST_Difficulties
[*]/Game/_Dawnwalker/System/Settings/ST_Settings_Tab_Game
[/list]
Difficulty overhauls and mods replacing these menu string tables can conflict, even when their archive filenames differ.
