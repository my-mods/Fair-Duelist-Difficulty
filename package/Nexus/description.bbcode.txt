[b]Fair Duelist Difficulty[/b]

Keep Duelist's short, deadly fights while giving yourself more room for mistakes. This mod keeps Duelist enemy health, aggression and action settings, but uses Fair enemy damage and combat stamina costs.

The existing Duelist option is renamed [b]Fair Duelist[/b]. This replaces that preset; it does not add a fifth difficulty.

[b]What changes[/b]
[list]
[*]Enemy health stays at Duelist: 90% of Fair.
[*]Enemy damage becomes Fair: 100%, instead of Duelist's 160%.
[*]Your combat stamina costs become Fair: 100%, instead of Duelist's 175%.
[*]Enemy aggression, attack speed and parry timing retain Duelist settings.
[*]Directional indicators remain disabled in the preset.
[*]The preset name and difficulty descriptions explain the new balance.
[/list]
Player maximum health and the other difficulty presets are unchanged.

[b]Requirements[/b]
The Blood of Dawnwalker for PC. No other mod, DLC or UE4SS loader is required.
For the installation steps below, use [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/site/mods/1]Vortex[/url] with its The Blood of Dawnwalker game extension.

[b]Install[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Download Fair-Duelist.zip and import it into Vortex.
[*]If asked for a mod type, choose Root (game folder).
[*]Enable the mod, deploy through Vortex, and restart the game.
[*]Select Fair Duelist in the game's difficulty settings.
[/list]

[b]Can I customize the difficulty?[/b]
Yes. Keep [b]RPG Difficulty on Fair Duelist[/b] to preserve the modified enemy health, damage and stamina costs.
Changing Action Difficulty changes aggression, attack speed and parry timing independently. Indicators and other custom options can also be changed. Seeing Custom as the overall preset does not remove the modified RPG balance.
For the exact preset described above, keep Action Difficulty on Fair Duelist and directional indicators off. Selecting another RPG difficulty or overall preset changes the balance accordingly.

[b]Update and uninstall[/b]
Update by replacing the same Vortex mod entry with the new Fair-Duelist.zip, then deploy and restart. Remove duplicate old entries through Vortex if needed.
To uninstall, disable or remove the mod in Vortex, deploy, and restart the game. The original Duelist settings and text are restored once no other mod overrides them.

[b]Compatibility and conflicts[/b]
Do not combine this with another mod replacing the same difficulty configuration or text tables unless you have chosen which version should win. Fair Duelist must win for its balance and wording to apply.
Affected assets:
[list]
[*]/Game/_Dawnwalker/Combat/DA_DifficultyConfig
[*]/Game/_Dawnwalker/Combat/StringTables/ST_Difficulties
[*]/Game/_Dawnwalker/System/Settings/ST_Settings_Tab_Game
[/list]
Difficulty overhauls and mods replacing these menu string tables can conflict, even when their archive filenames differ.
